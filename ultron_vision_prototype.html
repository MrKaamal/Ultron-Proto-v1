⚠ CRITICAL EEE GOLDEN RULE (Back-EMF Protection):
Never connect a 24V/48V motor driver GND directly to your laptop or Jetson USB port without an ADuM1201 / ISO1042 Galvanic Isolator and a TVS Diode + Shunt Brake Resistor on the DC bus. When a robotic arm decelerates rapidly, the motor acts as a generator and pumps high-voltage spikes back into the power rail.
